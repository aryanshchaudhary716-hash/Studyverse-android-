English – Noun (40 min)

Noun = naming word

Kisi person, place, thing ya idea ke naam ko Noun kehte hain.

Examples: Aryan (person), India (place), Book (thing), Honesty (idea).

Types: Proper (India), Common (city), Collective (team), Material (gold), Abstract (honesty).

Shorthand – Pitman first 8 strokes (90 min)

Pitman shorthand mein consonants ko strokes se likhte hain. Light aur heavy stroke ka difference important hai.
P / BSlanting downstroke ↘ · P light, B heavy
T / DVertical downstroke ↓ · T light, D heavy
CH / JOpposite slant downstroke ↙ · CH light, J heavy
K / GHorizontal stroke → · K light, G heavy

Practice: Har stroke 10 baar banao. Pen pressure, direction aur angle ka difference clear rakho. Exact angles ke liye Pitman chart follow karo.

Revision 40 min: Noun ke 10 examples likho, 10 number series solve karo, GK facts revise karo aur strokes repeat karo.
